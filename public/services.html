Manufacturing Services

Two flexible manufacturing models for schools, retailers and institutional buyers.

How We Work

Our Manufacturing Models

We offer two clearly structured manufacturing models to match different buyer needs. Choose the model that works best for your business.

01
Model 01 — Job-Work Manufacturing

You Supply. We Manufacture.

In our job-work model, your company supplies the fabric, lining, buttons, zippers and any other agreed materials. Our facility and workforce handle all the agreed manufacturing processes — cutting, stitching, finishing and quality checking — and return the completed garments to you.

1

Order Discussion & Specification Agreement

We discuss your garment requirements, sizing specifications and production quantities to agree on scope.

2

Customer Supplies Fabric & Materials to Our Facility

You deliver fabric rolls, trims, buttons, zippers, lining and any other agreed materials to our production unit.

3

Cutting & Pattern Making

Our team cuts fabric to pattern specifications, ensuring sizing accuracy and minimal material wastage.

4

Stitching & Assembly

Garments are stitched and assembled by our skilled workforce to agreed construction and finish standards.

5

Quality Check & Return of Finished Garments

Each batch is quality-checked against your specification before being packed and returned to you.

Ideal For

Retailers and institutions that have existing fabric sourcing arrangements and need a reliable manufacturing partner.

Our Standards

Quality & Process Standards

Consistent quality across every batch is a core part of how we operate — from the first cut to the final dispatch.

Cutting Precision

Fabric cutting is carried out carefully against approved patterns to ensure dimensional accuracy. Consistent piece sizes reduce variation across a production batch and help maintain sizing uniformity in the finished garment.

Stitching Strength

Seams are stitched to withstand the demands of regular school or institutional use. Stress points such as cuffs, collars and waistbands receive additional attention to ensure garments hold their shape and construction over time.

Measurement Consistency

Size specifications are documented and referenced at the production stage so that every garment in a batch conforms to the agreed measurements. This is especially important for institutional orders where uniformity across dozens or hundreds of pieces is expected.

Finishing Standards

Loose threads are trimmed, garments are pressed and checked for surface defects before packing. We aim to deliver garments that are ready for direct use or distribution without requiring additional finishing work at the buyer's end.

How It Works

Order Process

A simple, transparent process from your first enquiry to delivered garments — applicable to both manufacturing models.

01

Send Enquiry

Share your requirement via phone, WhatsApp, email or the online contact form. Tell us the garment type, approximate quantities and your location.

02

Requirement Discussion

We discuss your garment specifications in detail — fabric, sizing, colour, style, collar type, finish requirements and your preferred manufacturing model.

03

Quotation

You receive a detailed, transparent quotation covering per-piece pricing, minimum order quantity and estimated lead time for your requirement.

04

Sample Approval

A pre-production sample is made to your specification and submitted for your review and approval before bulk production begins.

05

Bulk Production

Once the sample is approved, full batch production begins. Quality checks are conducted during and after production against agreed specifications.

06

Delivery

Finished garments are packed and dispatched on the agreed schedule. Delivery is to your specified address or collected from our facility as arranged.

Model Comparison

Which Model Is Right for You?

Use this guide to identify which manufacturing model best fits your situation. You can always contact us to discuss your specific case.

Job-Work Manufacturing

Consider job-work if:

  • You already have an established fabric sourcing arrangement or preferred fabric supplier
  • You want to retain full control over material quality and sourcing
  • You are a retailer or distributor looking to outsource stitching and finishing to a skilled manufacturing partner
  • You are working with specific or branded fabric that you need to supply yourself
  • You want a focused manufacturing-only service and already handle procurement and logistics
Enquire About Job-Work
Complete Manufacturing

Consider complete manufacturing if:

  • You are a school or institution without an in-house procurement team for fabric and materials
  • You want a single vendor to manage the entire garment supply chain from start to finish
  • You prefer to give specifications and receive finished, ready-to-distribute garments
  • You require delivery to your location as part of the service arrangement
  • You are placing a bulk order and want all sourcing, production and QC handled by one team
