Our Products

School uniforms and institutional garments manufactured with consistent quality — built for durability, comfort and a professional finish.

What We Make

Garments Designed for Schools & Institutions

At Ayush Creation, every garment we manufacture is built for a B2B buyer — a school, a retailer or an institution. We focus on consistent sizing, durable construction and clean finishing across every batch. Products are available through both our job-work and complete manufacturing models.

Durable Construction

Reinforced seams and quality stitching ensure garments withstand daily school and institutional use over a full academic year.

Consistent Batch Quality

Each production batch is checked against an approved sample to ensure consistency in size, colour and finish across the entire order.

Custom Specifications

Colour, fabric type, collar style, pocket placement, branding — we work to your specification, not a generic template.

Bulk & Scheduled Delivery

We plan production around your institutional timelines — whether a single annual order or phased batch deliveries throughout the year.
