Who We Are

Who We Are

A dedicated garment manufacturing company based in Mumbai / Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra — built exclusively for B2B buyers.

Ayush Creation is a garment manufacturing company based in Mumbai / Navi Mumbai, Maharashtra. We specialise in manufacturing school uniforms and institutional garments for schools, uniform retailers, wholesalers and institutional buyers across the region and beyond.

We operate with two clearly defined manufacturing models. Under our Job-Work model, the customer supplies the fabric and agreed materials while we provide the manufacturing expertise, infrastructure and skilled workforce to convert them into finished garments. Under our Complete Manufacturing model, we manage the entire process end-to-end — from sourcing fabric to delivering finished, packaged garments — giving our clients a single-vendor, fully managed solution.

Ayush Creation is a strictly B2B operation. We work exclusively with institutional and trade buyers — including schools, uniform retailers, wholesalers and procurement teams. We do not sell direct to individual consumers.

Our Background

Our Story

Ayush Creation was founded in [FOUNDING_YEAR] by [FOUNDER_NAME] with a clear vision: to bring reliable, quality-focused garment manufacturing to institutional buyers in Mumbai. Recognising the need for a dedicated manufacturing partner who truly understood the requirements of schools, uniform retailers and institutional procurement — rather than a generalist factory juggling a wide range of clients — Ayush Creation was established with an exclusive focus on institutional and school garments.

From its early days, the company built its reputation on two fundamentals: consistent quality and dependable delivery. School uniforms and institutional garments carry a unique set of demands — specific fabrics, precise sizing across large batches, uniform finishing and strict adherence to colour and style standards. Ayush Creation developed its production workflow around these exact requirements, training its team and organising its processes to meet the expectations of institutional buyers across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and broader Maharashtra.

Today, Ayush Creation continues to serve schools, uniform retailers, wholesalers and institutional buyers with two structured manufacturing models: job-work (where the customer supplies fabric and we manufacture) and complete manufacturing (end-to-end, from fabric sourcing to finished garments). Our commitment remains unchanged — to be a manufacturing partner our clients can count on, season after season.
